## 💼 Real-World Impact Case Studies

How our technical features prevent real-world discrimination:

### 1. Eliminating Bias in Hiring & Salary (SDG 10.2 / 10.3)
**The Problem**: Many AI models used in modern HR (Talent Acquisition) inadvertently penalize female candidates due to historical salary data gaps.
**The Solution**: Our **Threshold Optimizer** and **SHAP Explainability** modules allow HR departments to audit their models. By mechanically enforcing "Demographic Parity," we ensure that qualified candidates receive identical starting scores regardless of protected attributes like Gender or Age.

### 2. Preventing "Proxy Discrimination" in Bank Loans (SDG 1.4 / 10.3)
**The Problem**: AI models often use "Proxy" variables—such as Zip Codes—which correlate heavily with Race or Origin. This leads to "Digital Redlining" where entire communities are denied credit based on demographic overlap.
**The Solution**: The platform's **Bivariate Explorer** acts as a Proxy Detector. It flags statistical correlations between seemingly innocent variables and forbidden demographics, forcing financial institutions to remove discriminatory features from their credit-scoring pipelines.
